My Experience

Alexandra Paige

That's me! I am a graphic designer, illustrator, and occasional website designer. It may come as a shock, but I, the developer of this lesbian themed website, am a lesbian. I have known I was a queer person from a young age but it wasn’t until recently where I realized I was a lesbian; better late than never!

I currently live in Southeast Wisconsin which is wedged right in between Madison, Milwaukee, and Chicago; and one thing that’s true about Wisconsin, we love our bars! So it only felt natural that I would be interested in a bar full of others just like me. What follows is my adventures to LGBT+ spaces from bars, to galleries, to cafes!

Club Icon

Club Icon is Kenosha Wisconsin's most well known LGBT space. I am no stranger to this place by any means and have been treated with nothing but kindness from the staff and fellow guests! The picture to the right showcases me and my partner after securing our win for the Halloween Couples Costume Contest! I am dressed up as Pearl from the movie Pearl and my partner is the scarecrow.

East View Coffee Co

East View Coffee Co is a cafe in downtown Kenosha! This establishment is LGBT+ friendly and has some of the best coffee in Kenosha. I had a lavender iced latte and my partner had a London fog tea; both were perfectly lovely drinks and I am excited to visit again!

This cafe also hosted a variety of art from within the community. A large portion coming from Kenosha’s Lemonstreet Gallery as well.
